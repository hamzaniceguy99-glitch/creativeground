How we work

Not a publisher, no rights taken

Creative Ground is coaching only. We take no equity, no revenue share and no rights in your project, and we're not a studio or funding body.

We also don't do legal or business advice. Engine licenses, asset licenses, contracts and store agreements matter and they belong with a lawyer, not with a coach who can only tell you what's typical.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Which engine should I use?

Whichever you'll stop evaluating and start building in. Engine switching is the most popular form of procrastination in this hobby.

I can't do art.

Then pick a style that works with that, or use licensed assets and check the licenses. Plenty of successful games look deliberately simple.

How long should my first game take?

Weeks, not years. Something tiny and finished teaches more than something ambitious and abandoned.

Will my game sell?

We don't know, and neither does anyone promising otherwise. We coach making and shipping; the market is its own thing.

Do you help with publishing contracts?

We'll tell you what's commonly in them and then send you to a lawyer. Signing one on coaching advice would be a bad idea.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
